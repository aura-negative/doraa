FAQ

Good to know

Is dhāgā free to use?

Yes — creating a profile, browsing matches, and messaging after a mutual match are all free.

Which platforms is it available on?

dhāgā is an Android app, currently in private testing ahead of its public Play Store listing.

Do you track my location?

No. We only ask for your country, state and district as text during onboarding — we never request or store GPS coordinates.

Do you verify profiles or run background checks?

No — we don't run identity or background checks on members. What we do give you is control: block anyone instantly (it works both ways), report a profile, a chat, or a single message for our team to review, and an 18+ minimum enforced at signup and again in our database.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Open Profile → Delete Account in the app, type your name to confirm, and it's gone immediately and permanently. If you can't sign in, email support@dhagamatrimony.in from your registered address and we'll do it for you. Full details are in our data deletion policy.

Who can I contact for support?

Reach us any time at support@dhagamatrimony.in.
