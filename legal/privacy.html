Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Who We Are

dhāgā is a matrimony and dating service. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the dhāgā mobile app and nothing else.

Information You Give Us

To create an account we collect and verify your mobile phone number. During onboarding you provide your name, date of birth, gender, and up to three photos. You may also provide your location (country, state, district and locality as text — we do not collect GPS or precise device location), height, marital status, mother tongue, religion, caste, education, profession, employer, income range and a short bio. Some of these are treated as sensitive information in India; you choose whether to provide them, and they are shown to other members as part of your profile.

Information Created By Using the App

We store the messages and photos you exchange with your matches, your likes, passes, matches, blocks, and any reports you submit. We also store a notification token for your device so we can deliver push notifications, along with your device platform.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics for product measurement and Crashlytics for diagnostics. This includes our sign-up, login, interest, match and message-sent events, automatically collected app and session events, and device, installation and diagnostic identifiers. We do not send message contents, photos, phone numbers or profile attributes as event parameters. Events and crash reports may be linked to your pseudonymous account ID while signed in. Advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled. Resetting analytics on your device does not erase historical server records; contact support for a data-deletion request.

How We Use Your Information

We use your profile to show you to other members and to show you profiles matching your preferences. We use your messages to deliver them to their recipients. We use your contact details and device token to send service and match notifications. We use reports, blocks and message content to investigate safety complaints and enforce our Terms. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who We Share It With

Other members see the profile information and photos you choose to publish, and the messages you send them. Beyond that we share data only with the service providers that run the app: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), APITXT (delivery of one-time login codes by SMS), and Google Firebase (push notifications, analytics, crash reporting). APITXT receives your phone number and one-time code only to deliver the requested login message. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the safety of our members.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in their Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region. Your messages are also cached on your own device so the app works offline; clearing the app data or uninstalling removes that local copy.

Photos You Send

Profile photos are displayed to other members. Chat photos currently use public, unlisted URLs with random filenames. Anyone who obtains a URL can open it until the object is removed and caches expire. Blocking or unmatching does not revoke an existing photo URL. Treat a photo you send as something the recipient can keep.

How Long We Keep It

Your account is disabled when a deletion request is accepted. Active account data, including your profile, messages and owned media, is erased within seven days. Minimized safety cases may be retained while open; closed cases are deleted after 90 days unless a documented legal hold applies. We review open cases and legal holds regularly. Deletion receipts are kept for 90 days for operational verification and restoration safeguards. Analytics, aggregate reports, Crashlytics records and provider backups have separate lifecycles and are not covered by the seven-day active-data promise. Contact support@dhagamatrimony.in for current provider retention details or historical analytics deletion.

Deleting Your Account

Request deletion from Profile → Delete Account. Confirm using your name, or DELETE if your profile is incomplete. We disable account access immediately after acceptance, show a request number and completion deadline, and finish deletion in the background. This is irreversible. If you cannot sign in, email support@dhagamatrimony.in; after verifying ownership we submit the same deletion request. Media URLs and cached copies may remain accessible until removal and cache expiry. We cannot erase copies independently kept by another member.

Your Rights

You can view and correct most of your information directly in the app under Profile → Edit Profile. You may also ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or erase it, or withdraw consent for its processing, by writing to support@dhagamatrimony.in. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Safety and Moderation

If another member reports your profile or a message, our team may review the reported content to keep the community safe. We may remove content and suspend or terminate accounts that break our Terms of Service.

Children

dhāgā is strictly for adults. The service is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor we delete it. If you believe a minor is using the app, report the profile in the app or email support@dhagamatrimony.in.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to the database is restricted per-account by server-side rules, and credentials are stored using your device secure storage. No service can promise perfect security. Never share a one-time login code with anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised, and we will notify you in the app about significant changes.

Contact Us

For any privacy question, request or complaint, contact us at support@dhagamatrimony.in and we will respond as quickly as we can.
